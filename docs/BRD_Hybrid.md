# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Name
AI Marketing Studio

## Vision
A multi-tenant AI-powered marketing platform that enables businesses to create brand-specific AI marketing agents.

## Frontend Strategy
### Hybrid Approach

#### Public Website
Technology: Next.js

Purpose:
- SEO-optimized marketing website
- Landing pages
- Pricing pages
- Documentation
- Blog content
- Lead generation

#### Application Portal
Technology: Angular

Purpose:
- Brand Management
- Product Management
- Campaign Management
- Analytics Dashboard
- AI Agent Configuration
- Subscription Management

## Business Goals
- Automate content creation
- Automate marketing workflows
- Support multiple brands
- Build a SaaS platform
- Increase marketing efficiency
